# Hotwords: contextual biasing in the decoder

Names, terms and acronyms a model mishears as common words (`Губернетис` for `кубернетес`,
`GigaM` for `GigaAM`) are favoured inside the decoder, through `Options::hotwords`
(`Hotwords { words, boost, beam }`) and `Model::takes_hotwords`. The caller's app: wavoscribe's
personal dictionary (its plan `docs/plans/20261121-hotword-biasing.md`).

Design: the words are a tree of lowercase characters, matched on the text of the pieces, so a
word gets its bonus however the vocabulary cuts it (BPE or characters). A hypothesis gets
`boost` per matching character from a word's start; it keeps it at the word's end or stem, plus
up to 3 Cyrillic letters of a case ending (`докером`), and loses it when the word turns off
(`доктор` for `докер`). A phrase also matches with its words run together. The beam keeps the
likeliest alignment per prefix (max, not the sum of prefix beam search), so without a match it
ends on greedy decoding's transcript; `None` or no words runs the greedy code itself.

Pruning: a partial bonus counts in full for 3 frames after the word's last letter, then half as
much per frame, and each frame also keeps the `beam / 2` likeliest hypotheses without any
partial bonus. Without both, a hypothesis that had matched `парак` stayed on blanks over the
next words and pushed the others out of the beam (`паракверь` for `паракет и проверь` on
`ru-terms`, 26 s in pieces); the `an_unfinished_word_drops_no_words_after_it` test decodes `док`
without them. A lone `▁` right before a word start is dropped (the model writes one before Latin
letters; the bonus can then spell the word in Cyrillic, which gave a double space).

- [x] CTC (GigaAM `e2e-ctc`, `ctc`, multilingual): beam search over raw logits with the bonus,
      token frames from a Viterbi alignment of the result; unit tests on synthetic logits
      (near-miss, inflection, roll-back, phrase, character vocabulary, greedy without a match).
- [x] `gigaam_v3_e2e_ctc` model test: unheard hotwords leave the samples' transcripts as greedy
      writes them, text and tokens.
- [x] No dropped words: partial-bonus decay and the settled half of the beam, with a test that
      fails without them.
- [x] A phrase that turns off past a space ends there: the words before keep their bonus and the
      next word starts from the root, not as their case ending. With `докер` and `докер компоуз`,
      `докер образ` came out `докер обз` (`обз` fit the 3 letters of an ending, `образ` didn't);
      `a_word_after_a_phrase_that_turned_off_is_no_case_ending` fails without it.
- [x] Transducers, GigaAM RNN-T (`e2e-rnnt`, `rnnt`) and Parakeet TDT (`transducer`, one search
      for both: RNN-T is TDT whose tokens stay on their frame and whose blank moves one on). Paths
      are compared on the same frame only, merged per prefix (the predictor's state follows from
      the prefix). Unlike CTC's, a transducer's beam over all tokens finds paths greedy decoding
      doesn't take in everyday speech (`к 9:00` came out `к девяти`), so a path leaves greedy
      decoding's decision only for a word: off one it makes greedy's decision or starts a word;
      spelling one, it may also wait for the next letter (at most `GRACE` frames) or take the
      letter that goes on with it. Such a guess ends when its word turns off before its end, and
      a token below greedy's must pay for most of its cost with its own bonus (`REACH`, 4): else
      a guess spells a whole word out on one frame, whatever was said. The best path that is no
      guess always stays in the beam. Half CTC's beam (4): each path costs a predictor step and a
      joint row, both batched per frame (`matvec_each` for Parakeet's 8193-token joint).
- [x] Model tests: unheard hotwords leave `gigaam-v3-e2e-rnnt`'s and `parakeet-ultra`'s samples
      as greedy writes them, text and tokens (token frames are each path's own, not the prefix's
      first: a guess emitted `▁до` a frame earlier than greedy). Unit tests on a scripted
      transducer as RNN-T and as TDT: the word found, greedy without one or with an unheard one,
      and `REACH` (fails without it).

Measured on the Mac (CPU backend), gigaam-v3-e2e-ctc, beam 8, macOS `say`'s Milena voice:
5 clips with terms, alone and as wavoscribe's `ru-terms` fixture (26 s, cut at its pauses), and
20 everyday sentences near the terms without them (`доктор`, `доке`, `кубок`, `пароль`,
`паровоз`, `гигантские`), alone and as `ru-general` (113 s). Wavoscribe's
`scripts/fixtures.py` makes both.

| Boost | Clips fixed | `ru-terms` fixed | Everyday text changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5 (default) | `Губернетис` → `кубернетес`, `GigaM` → `GigaAM`, `паракет` → `паракит`, `доkrbraz` → `докер обraz` | `Doкer` → `докере`, `Gigam` → `GigaAm` | none of 21 |
| 1.75 | same | also `Gubrnetis` → `кубернетес`, `паракет` → `паракит`, `Jason` → `Джейсон` | `доке` → `докере` |
| 2 | same | as 1.75 but `паракет` | `доке` → `докере` |

Not fixed at any boost: `VгоSкrap`/`Vevo Scrape` (Wavoscribe), `PSSH` (по SSH).

Speed: 25 s of random logits (1025 classes, no row peaked: the worst case) decode in 5.3 ms at
beam 8 vs 0.6 ms greedy; on the model's own logits the difference is within the run's noise
(~1 ms per 3–5 s clip, 26 s of `ru-terms` in 694 vs 680 ms end to end).

Transducers, measured on the Mac (CPU), wavoscribe's built-in IT terms (100 terms, 160 spellings)
at boost 1.5; its reading page `ru-it` (206 s, 98 terms), `ru-terms` and `ru-general`:

|---|---|---|---|---|

| `parakeet-ultra` | 59 → 72 | unchanged | `GigaAM`, `Parakeet` | +8 ms per s |
| `gigaam-v3-e2e-ctc` (for comparison) | 16 → 71 | unchanged | | |

Tuning, GigaAM RNN-T: beam 8 found 66 terms in twice beam 4's time; `REACH` 2 found 56, 6 found
70 at 4× the cost; before `REACH`, every frame ran its 10 rounds and the search took 8× greedy.
